Antenna Workbench

Yagi Antenna Design & Analysis

Design, analyze, and optimize Yagi antennas for CB and amateur radio. Full NEC-based physics engine with SWR, gain, radiation patterns, and impedance matching.

Get The App Back to Home

What You Can Do

2-20 Element Designs

Build from simple 2-element beams to massive 20-element long-boom Yagis. Reflectors, driven elements, and directors.

SWR Analysis

Real-time SWR curves with 201-point frequency sweep. See usable bandwidth at 1.5:1 and 2.0:1 thresholds.

Radiation Patterns

Polar plots for azimuth and elevation. Front/back ratio, beamwidth, and takeoff angle visualization.

Gamma & Hairpin Match

Built-in gamma match and hairpin match designers. Full impedance calculations with Smith chart visualization.

Auto-Tune & Optimization

One-click optimization of element lengths and spacings. Fine-tune for best SWR, gain, or front/back ratio.

Stacking & Arrays

Model stacked arrays with automatic spacing optimization. Vertical, horizontal, and quad configurations.
